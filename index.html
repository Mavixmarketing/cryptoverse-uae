Questions

What founders ask us first

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The 30-minute discovery call is free and is for us to understand what you're building. It is not legal advice. Advice, including the consultation and written memo on your route and costs, is billed at our hourly rates, which we confirm before any work starts.

How much does a crypto licence in the UAE cost?

For VARA, regulator fees start at AED 40,000 to apply and AED 80,000 a year per activity, with paid-up capital from AED 100,000. Exchanges, broker-dealers and custodians pay more. On top come company setup, office, staff and our professional fees, which we quote in writing.

How long does it take?

Most VARA licences take 6 to 12 months from first filing. A complete, well-drafted application and fast answers to the regulator are what keep it on the short end. Nobody can honestly promise a faster approval, and the decision is always the regulator's.

VARA, ADGM or DIFC: which should I choose?

It depends on where you want to operate, whether your clients are retail or institutional, what you'll do and how you'll bank. In the consultation we compare the routes that fit your model and recommend one, with reasons.

Do I need a UAE company first?

Usually yes, but the right entity depends on the route. We set up the company in the right order so you don't form one you later have to change.

Can you help with a bank account?

We prepare your onboarding pack and introduce you to banks and EMIs that work with licensed crypto firms. Whether to open the account is the bank's decision, so we cannot promise one.
