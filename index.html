Questions

What founders ask us first

How much does a crypto licence in the UAE cost?

For VARA, regulator fees start at AED 40,000 to apply and AED 80,000 a year per activity, with paid-up capital from AED 100,000. Exchanges and custodians pay more. On top come company setup, office, staff and our legal fees. Message us your activities and we'll send you a full first-year estimate.

How long does it take?

Most VARA licences take 6 to 12 months from first filing. A complete, well-drafted application and fast answers to the regulator are what keep it on the short end. Nobody can honestly promise a faster approval.

VARA, ADGM or DIFC: which should I choose?

It depends on where you want to operate, whether your clients are retail or institutional, what you'll do and how you'll bank. We compare the routes that fit your model and recommend one, with reasons.

Do I need a UAE company first?

Usually yes, but the right entity depends on the route. We set up the company in the right order so you don't form one you later have to change.

Can you help with a bank account?

Yes. We prepare your onboarding pack and introduce you to banks, EMIs and exchanges that work with licensed crypto firms.
