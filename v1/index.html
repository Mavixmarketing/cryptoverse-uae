Why founders are asking now

2026 changed the rules for unlicensed crypto businesses

Three shifts this year mean that "we'll sort the licence later" is getting expensive.

September 2026

The Central Bank's grace period ended

The UAE's new Central Bank law (Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025) gave firms one year to come into line. That window closed in mid-September 2026. Payment services that use virtual assets now sit squarely under CBUAE licensing.

Source: Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025
21 October 2026

Google now asks crypto advertisers for a licence

Exchanges and wallets can now advertise to UAE users only with a VARA, CMA, DFSA or FSRA licence. No licence means no Google ads, so growth plans now start with the licence.

Source: Google Ads cryptocurrency policy, UAE
November 2026

Only 61 firms hold an active VARA licence

New rulebooks this year (derivatives, issuance, AML risk assessment) raised the bar again. Thin applications get sent back; a complete, well-drafted pack is what gets through.

Source: VARA public register, checked 16 Nov 2026

Questions

What founders ask before the call

How much does a crypto licence in the UAE cost?

It depends on the regulator, your activities and your structure. Costs come from regulator application and annual fees, minimum capital, company setup, and the documents and compliance team you need.

Your comparison sheet lists the current regulator fees and capital for each route, and we price our work for your model on the call.

How long does it take?

Usually several months from first filing to final licence, depending on the regulator and how complete the application is. The biggest delays come from thin applications and slow answers to regulator questions. Both are the part we take off your hands.

VARA, ADGM or DIFC: how do I choose?

It comes down to where you want to operate, who your clients are (retail or institutional), what you will do, and how you plan to bank. On the call we compare the routes that fit your model and recommend one, with reasons.

Do I need a UAE company before I apply?

Usually yes, but the right entity depends on the route. We set up the structure in the right order so you don't form a company you later have to change.

Can you help with a bank account?

Yes. We prepare you for bank, EMI and exchange onboarding and introduce you to institutions that work with licensed crypto firms. No one can promise a bank will say yes, but a clean licence file makes it far more likely.

What happens on the free call?

Twenty minutes with a lawyer. We ask about your model, markets and timing, tell you which route fits and why, and what it will roughly cost. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Your answers go only to our legal team. We never share or sell your details.
