Why it happens

Most freezes are compliance reviews, not the end of the road

Exchanges and banks freeze funds when a transfer trips their checks. What gets them released is usually the right documents, sent the right way, to the right people.

Common trigger

Source-of-funds questions

A large or unusual deposit, and the platform wants proof of where the money came from. Weak answers keep the account locked.

Common trigger

A flagged counterparty

Funds passed through a wallet or account the platform's screening tools flagged, often without you knowing.

Common trigger

Bank concerns about crypto

Your bank sees transfers to or from an exchange and restricts the account while it reviews them.

Questions

What people ask before the call

Why did the exchange freeze my account?

Most often a compliance review: a source-of-funds question, a flagged wallet or counterparty, a KYC update, or a legal request. The platform's messages and terms usually show which one, and that decides the next step.

How long does it take to get funds released?

It depends on the platform, the reason and how fast the right documents reach them. Some cases move quickly once the compliance team has what it needs; others need a formal notice or a regulator complaint. We tell you what to expect on the first call.

Can you guarantee my funds will be released?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. What we can do is put your case to the platform properly and push it through the right channels.

What does it cost?

The first review call is free. If you go ahead, we quote a clear fee for your case before we start.

Do you help if I was scammed?

We don't offer fund recovery or tracing for scam losses. If your funds are held by a regulated exchange or bank, we can help you deal with them.

I'm not in the UAE. Can you still help?

Often, yes, especially where the exchange or bank is UAE-based or licensed here. Tell us where you and the platform are, and we'll say straight away.
